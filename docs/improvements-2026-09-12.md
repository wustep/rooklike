## Technical

### 1. Reuse one AI worker instead of spawning one per enemy move
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S · **Status:** 🕓 deferred
- **Problem.** `src/App.tsx:56-60` builds a new module Worker inside the effect and terminates it on cleanup, so every enemy turn (and every toggle of `intro`/`help`/`restart`, which are deps) re-boots the worker and re-imports `game.ts` + chess.js. The 550ms timer restarts too, so opening Help mid-think delays the reply.
- **Proposal.** Hold the worker in a ref, create lazily once, post `{id, run}`, ignore replies whose id is not the latest. Keep the `current===run` guard. Terminate on unmount only.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Stale replies must be rejected by id or a takeback could be overwritten.
- **Files.** `src/App.tsx`, `src/ai.worker.ts`

### 2. Trusted replay path in `getChess`
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** M · **Status:** 🕓 deferred
- **Problem.** `src/game.ts:118` replays `run.moves` through `legalMove`; `src/rules.ts:26-32` clones a Chess and runs two `assertPosition` scans per ply. A 40-ply battle costs 40 FEN round-trips, on every run change, every selection (`riskySquares`), and inside `playMove`.
- **Proposal.** Replay stored SANs with `chess.move`, reject king captures, `assertPosition` once at the end. Early-return `riskySquares` when no legal moves. Memoize `routeMap`.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Weaker validation of tampered saves; final assert plus king-capture check keep the invariants.
- **Files.** `src/game.ts`, `src/rules.ts`, `src/App.tsx`

### 3. Transposition table keyed on full FEN, never replaces
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S · **Status:** 🕓 deferred
- **Problem.** `src/engine.ts:111` keys on `chess.fen()` including clocks, so transpositions miss. `engine.ts:121` stops writing at 20000 entries instead of replacing.
- **Proposal.** Key on the first four FEN fields plus captain; depth-preferred replacement or clear-and-refill.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** 50-move scores slightly approximate deep in the tree. `tests/depth.test.ts` pins move quality.
- **Files.** `src/engine.ts`

### 4. Worker-failure fallback ignores difficulty and can white-screen
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** 🕓 deferred
- **Problem.** `src/App.tsx:58` falls back to `chooseMove(..., 'wanderer')` synchronously on the main thread regardless of difficulty; no try/catch around `playMove` in either handler.
- **Proposal.** Pass `run.difficulty`, cap with a tiny override, wrap handlers and surface `setNotice`.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Weaker move on the rare error path.
- **Files.** `src/App.tsx`

### 5. `loadRun` can silently re-roll the seed
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** 🕓 deferred
- **Problem.** `src/game.ts:205` spreads `newRun()` (seed `Date.now()`) under the saved object; a save without `seed` gets a fresh seed that disagrees with its persisted `initialFen`/`elite`. No finite-number or difficulty-enum guards.
- **Proposal.** Scalar `DEFAULTS` object, deterministic seed fallback, `Number.isFinite` and enum guards.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Stricter validation may route a save through the existing rebuild branch.
- **Files.** `src/game.ts`

### 6. Error boundary around the board
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** 🕓 deferred
- **Problem.** `getChess(run)` runs during render; any throw unmounts the tree with no UI (`src/main.tsx:5`).
- **Proposal.** Small class boundary offering "Rebuild position" and "New journey"; log the error.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Can mask bugs; keep the console log.
- **Files.** `src/main.tsx`, new component

### 7. Swift passage predicate computed twice and inconsistently
- **Impact:** ⚪ low · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `src/game.ts:149` pays at `turns<=par` while `bonusState` (`game.ts:88`) shows it lost on the exact par turn during battle.
- **Proposal.** One shared `swiftPassage(run)` predicate.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Update `tests/depth.test.ts:26`.
- **Files.** `src/game.ts`, `src/App.tsx`

## UI/UX & Design

### 8. Announce the enemy's move and check to screen readers
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** `.turn-bar` text (`src/App.tsx:153`) has no live region; only `.capture-feedback` and `.position-read` carry `role="status"`. A non-sighted player never hears the enemy move or check.
- **Proposal.** `role="status" aria-live="polite"` on the turn bar plus a visually hidden plain-language line built from `lastMove` and `chess.isCheck()`.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Double announcements if both regions fire; gate capture text to one source.
- **Files.** `src/App.tsx`, `src/style.css`

### 9. Show why it is check: highlight the checking piece
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** Only the king square is painted (`.king-check`, `src/style.css:34`); nothing points at the checker. `chess.attackers` is already used for `enemyReach` (`src/App.tsx:162`).
- **Proposal.** `.checking-piece` class on the attackers of the king square; optional ray via the existing `.move-trail` SVG.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Knight checks have no ray; ray must not hide legal dots.
- **Files.** `src/App.tsx`, `src/style.css`

### 10. Distinguish hanging from defended in the Threats overlay
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** M · **Status:** 🕓 deferred
- **Problem.** Every attacked square gets the same `.threat-dot` (`src/App.tsx:163`), so a defended pawn and a hanging queen look identical.
- **Proposal.** Compare attacker vs defender counts; `.threat-dot.hanging` filled vs `.defended` hollow; mirror in `aria-label`.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Count heuristic is not SEE; say "undefended", never "safe".
- **Files.** `src/App.tsx`, `src/style.css`

### 11. Animate the piece along the move (FLIP)
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** M · **Status:** 🕓 deferred
- **Problem.** `piece-arrive` drops the piece in place (`src/style.css:35`); enemy moves teleport. A dead `piece-land` rule at `style.css:11` is overridden.
- **Proposal.** Translate from the origin square via a CSS custom property, transition to 0 over ~180ms; delete the dead rule. Reduced-motion guard already covers the selector.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Castling moves two pieces; knights slide diagonally.
- **Files.** `src/style.css`, `src/App.tsx`

### 12. Sticky hover state on touch devices
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** No `@media(hover:hover)` anywhere; `.square:hover` ring and piece lift stay stuck after a tap on the 390px viewport, competing with `.selected`.
- **Proposal.** Wrap hover affordances in `@media(hover:hover)`; clear `hovered` on touch pointerup.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Verify the inspect panel still has a path on touch (tap-to-select does).
- **Files.** `src/style.css`, `src/App.tsx`

### 13. Arrow-key focus wraps across files at the edges
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** `src/App.tsx:166` clamps the flat index, so ArrowLeft on a1 lands on h2 and ArrowUp on rank 8 jumps to a8.
- **Proposal.** Block horizontal moves across file boundaries; no-op vertical moves out of range.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** None.
- **Files.** `src/App.tsx`

### 14. Drag ghost is a fixed 72×80px
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** `.drag-ghost` (`src/style.css:59`) is absolute while squares are fluid; on mobile the ghost is twice the square size and misaligned.
- **Proposal.** Measure the square from `squareRefs` at `beginDrag`, pass width as a CSS variable, scale ~1.08x.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Re-measure each drag (one `getBoundingClientRect`).
- **Files.** `src/App.tsx`, `src/style.css`

### 15. Material balance strip
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** M · **Status:** 🕓 deferred

### 16. Board does not communicate input lock while the AI thinks
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
